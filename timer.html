Focus time

Which length should I pick?

  • 25 min: classic Pomodoro. Good for starting hard tasks or when your focus is low.
  • 50 min: good for lectures, readings and problem sets.
  • 1 hr 30 min: deep work, like debugging a big program. A 15-minute break follows.
  • After a focus block the break length is picked for you: 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
  • Your chosen length and today's session count are saved in this browser.
  • The remaining time also shows in the browser tab, so you can work in another window.
