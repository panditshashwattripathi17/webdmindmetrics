    Quick answers

    Where is my data stored?

    In your browser's localStorage. Clearing site data or switching browsers starts you fresh.

    Why can't I see my data on another device?

    There is no server, so nothing syncs. Each browser keeps its own copy.

    How is the stress score worked out?

    It combines stress, mood, sleep, workload, energy and exercise. The About page lists the weights.

    Is this medical advice?

    No. It is a self-tracking tool for students. For real concerns, talk to a professional.
