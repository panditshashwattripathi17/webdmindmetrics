About MindMetrics

A small, honest tool that helps students notice stress early and study in a more balanced way.

Why we built it

Deadlines, labs and new routines pile up fast in first year. Most of us only notice the stress once it has already won.

MindMetrics turns a vague feeling into a number you can watch. Once you can see a pattern, such as short sleep before heavy weeks, you can plan around it.

How the score works

Each check-in becomes a 0–100 stress score. Lower is better.

  • Stress level counts for up to 30 points
  • Mood up to 20, and sleep up to 20 (8 hours or more adds nothing)
  • Workload and low energy up to 15 each
  • Exercise subtracts up to 5 (30 minutes earns the full amount)
  • Below 34 is Low, 34 to 66 is Moderate, 67 and above is High

Built with

  • HTML5 semantic pages with labelled forms
  • CSS3 custom properties, Grid, Flexbox and media queries
  • JavaScript (ES6+) modules of small functions, no frameworks
  • localStorage to keep your data in your browser
  • GitHub Pages for hosting

Good to know

This is a front-end project, so there is no server. Accounts and check-ins are stored in your own browser and are never sent anywhere.

MindMetrics is not a medical tool. If stress feels unmanageable, please talk to a friend, a mentor or your campus counsellor.
